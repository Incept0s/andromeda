## Libraries compiled in

Andromeda's Rust code depends on crates from [crates.io](https://crates.io),
under MIT, Apache-2.0, MPL-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unicode-3.0, BSL-1.0 and
CC0 licences. The complete list with versions and licences is generated by
`scripts/third-party.sh` into [THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md](THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md).

Notable ones: [Tauri](https://tauri.app) (MIT/Apache-2.0),
[Leptos](https://leptos.dev) (MIT), [zbus](https://github.com/dbus2/zbus)
(MIT), [libpulse-binding](https://github.com/jnqnfe/pulse-binding-rust)
(MIT/Apache-2.0), [nvml-wrapper](https://github.com/rust-nvml/nvml-wrapper)
(MIT/Apache-2.0), [rqrr](https://github.com/WanzenBug/rqrr)
(MIT/Apache-2.0 and ISC).
